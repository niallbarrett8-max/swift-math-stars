Math Stars – Daily Challenge Progression & End-of-Game Flow Improvements

Please implement the following two improvements while preserving all existing gameplay functionality.

⸻

1. DAILY CHALLENGE PROGRESSION

⸻

Current issue:

Daily Challenge stars appear to be shared across Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulties.

Desired behaviour:

Each difficulty should have its own independent daily progression.

Easy:

* Bronze
* Silver
* Gold

Medium:

* Bronze
* Silver
* Gold

Hard:

* Bronze
* Silver
* Gold

This allows players to earn stars separately for each difficulty level.

IMPORTANT:

Please review the Daily Challenge calendar and progress displays.

Do not simply add nine visible stars to each day if it creates clutter.

Goal:

* Keep the calendar clean and easy to read.
* Preserve a polished appearance.
* Design an intuitive way to show daily progress across all three difficulties.
* If necessary, show summary progress in the calendar and detailed progress when a day is selected.

Use your best judgement to create a clean, attractive solution.

⸻

2. END-OF-GAME RESULTS EXPERIENCE

⸻

Current issue:

When the timer reaches zero, the results screen appears immediately.

Because gameplay encourages rapid answering right until the final second, players may accidentally press:

* Play Again
* Main Menu

before they have a chance to review their results.

Desired behaviour:

When time reaches zero:

1. Brief “Time’s Up!” transition.
2. Show the results screen.
3. Animate key statistics counting upward to their final values:
    * Total Correct
    * Accuracy %
    * Best Streak
    * Any other key statistics shown
4. After the animation completes, reveal:
    * Play Again
    * Main Menu

Goals:

* Prevent accidental taps.
* Give players a moment to reflect on their performance.
* Make results feel rewarding.
* Improve the overall polish of the game experience.

Keep the animation smooth and relatively quick (approximately 1–2 seconds total).
