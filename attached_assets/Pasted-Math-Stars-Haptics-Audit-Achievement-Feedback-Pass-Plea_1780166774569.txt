Math Stars – Haptics Audit & Achievement Feedback Pass

Please audit and improve the haptic feedback system throughout the app.

Current issue:

Haptics are enabled in Settings, but on iPhone there is little or no noticeable vibration feedback during normal use.

Before making changes, inspect the current implementation and determine exactly how haptics are currently working.

⸻

PART 1 – HAPTICS AUDIT

Please investigate and report:

1. Which app events currently trigger haptics.
2. Which haptic API or implementation is currently being used.
3. Whether the current implementation supports:
    * Browser mode
    * PWA mode
    * Native iOS app builds
4. Any limitations that would prevent strong haptic feedback while testing through Replit preview on iPhone.
5. Whether haptics will behave differently once exported as a native iOS app.

⸻

PART 2 – HAPTIC IMPROVEMENTS

After completing the audit, improve the haptic experience using native mobile haptic patterns where possible.

Desired behaviour:

Button Presses:

* Very light feedback.
* Subtle and responsive.
* Should not feel distracting.

Number Entry:

* Same very light feedback.

Correct Answer:

* Positive success-style haptic.
* Noticeably different from a normal button press.

Incorrect Answer:

* Error-style haptic.
* Slightly more noticeable than a normal button press.

Bronze Star Earned:

* Success haptic.

Silver Star Earned:

* Success haptic.

Gold Star Earned:

* Success haptic.

Daily Challenge Completion:

* Most rewarding success haptic in the app.

Requirements:

* Use native haptic patterns whenever possible.
* Avoid long vibration bursts.
* Avoid repetitive buzzing.
* Focus on clean, modern mobile feedback.
* Keep the experience premium and subtle.

⸻

PART 3 – ACHIEVEMENT SOUNDS

Please add a reward sound family for achievement moments.

Apply to:

* Bronze Star Earned
* Silver Star Earned
* Gold Star Earned
* Daily Challenge Completed
* Major progression unlocks

Desired sound characteristics:

* Short
* Positive
* Clean
* Modern
* Rewarding
* Under 1 second

Avoid:

* Arcade sounds
* Casino sounds
* Cartoon sounds
* Loud celebrations
* Overly dramatic effects

The goal is to create a polished achievement feeling without becoming repetitive during long play sessions.

⸻

DO NOT CHANGE

Do not modify:

* Music system
* Music volume controls
* Button/key sound system
* Button/key sound volume controls
* Game rules
* Scoring
* Timers
* Progression systems
